Research

From a microwave signal to a better forecast

Over sea ice, satellite radiances are hard to use: the surface emits strongly and variably, and models misjudge the temperature of the layer that actually emits. My work follows the whole chain, from understanding the observation to measuring its effect on model analyses and forecasts.

  1. 01 · Observe Passive microwave Sounders and imagers such as ATMS, MHS and AMSR2, plus satellite-derived sea-ice products from OSI SAF.
  2. 02 · Characterize Sea-ice emissivity Estimating microwave surface emissivity and the effective emitting-layer temperature of sea ice and snow.
  3. 03 · Pre-process Screening & bias correction Cloud screening, bias correction and observation-error treatment tailored to polar conditions.
  4. 04 · Assimilate & evaluate Models & diagnostics Data-assimilation experiments in NWP and sea-ice models, and evaluation of analyses and forecasts.

Selected work

OSI SAF · DMI2025 – present

OSI SAF sea-ice products in HARMONIE

Assessing OSI SAF sea-ice products for NWP. I built a pan-Arctic hourly sea-ice product from OSI SAF Level 2 data and applied it in HARMONIE, DMI's operational NWP model. Initial evaluations are promising; a first-author manuscript is in preparation.

Postdoc · SNU2024 – present

Sea-ice data assimilation for climate models

Assimilating satellite-derived sea-ice concentration into CICE5 and SI3. With DART, I identified an optimal localization radius for Arctic sea-ice initialization. I am building a PDAF-based framework for SI3, the sea-ice component of KMA's coupled climate model, including observation operator, background-error covariance and ensemble perturbations.

Ph.D. · Ewha2018 – 2024

ATMS over winter sea ice in KMA's KIM

I showed that the main source of observed-minus-simulated radiance error was the mismatch between model surface temperature and the effective emitting-layer temperature, followed by emissivity errors. I proposed a bias correction for winter Arctic sea ice and tested it for operational use in the Korean Integrated Model.
